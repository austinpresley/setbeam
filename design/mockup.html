About this concept and its sample image

This is an interactive design mockup, not a working camera application. The portrait is fictional and was generated with the built-in image tool. QR codes use example data. Camera compatibility has not been hardware-tested.

Final sample-image prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: sample camera frame inside a polished professional Mac app mockup, not the interface itself. Create one photorealistic wide 16:9 editorial studio portrait photograph. A fictional adult woman in her early thirties wearing a crisp white shirt, seated naturally on a simple dark wooden stool, three-quarter body framing, photographed on a quiet pale gray seamless studio backdrop. Soft large-window light from camera left, subtle warm skin tones, honest photographic skin detail, gentle shadow falloff, premium restrained commercial portrait photography. Subject a little left of center with natural negative space to the right, cinematic but realistic, 50mm photographic lens feel. No text, no logos, no watermark, no interface, no borders, no camera equipment visible. This is fictional demonstration footage, not a real person or a reference identity.
