# Owned wireless-video targets

Researched 2026-10-20. Austin reports owning an original Hollyland Mars 300, a Hollyland Mars 300 Pro, and an Accsoon CineView Master 4K. Ownership and model names are user-reported; exact variants and firmware have not been inspected. This note uses first-party documentation only. No hardware tests, installs, configuration changes, or vendor contact occurred.

These three devices should define the first compatibility milestone. Prove a working Mac input for each before investing in packaging or claiming brand-wide support.

| Owned device | What the documentation establishes | Recommended route and next proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original Hollyland Mars 300 | Hollyland's legacy comparison lists it separately from the 300 Pro and describes HDMI input/loopout and dual HDMI output. The table appears to reverse TX/RX labels, so it should not establish exact port placement. No first-party direct Wi-Fi/app/RTSP route was established in this bounded review. [Legacy Hollyland comparison](https://hollyland-techhelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360062140573-Detailed-Comparison-Mars-Series) | Treat receiver HDMI into a compatible capture device as the first candidate. Confirm the unit's labels and receiver output, then demonstrate picture and sound on the Mac. Moderate confidence in the documented HDMI topology; capture compatibility remains untested. |
| Hollyland Mars 300 Pro | Hollyland documents joining the transmitter's Wi-Fi and opening HollyView. The manual describes receiver HDMI output. Its FAQ explicitly says Type-C-to-RJ45 streaming is unsupported. These sources do not establish a generic Mac RTSP endpoint. [HollyView instructions](https://www.hollyland.com/faq/how-to-connect-mars-300-pro-with-the-hollyview-app), [manual](https://download.hollyland.com/User_manual/MARS%20300PRO/Mars%20300%20Pro%20-%20User%20Manual%20%28English%29%20v1.1.0.pdf), [streaming FAQ](https://www.hollyland.com/support/faq/mars-300pro/others-3) | Prioritize a bounded direct Wi-Fi compatibility investigation on the owned unit. Keep receiver HDMI capture as the practical fallback. Record exact revision and firmware; Hollyland publishes separate Standard and Enhance firmware entries. [Variant distinction](https://www.hollyland.com/download/mars-300-pro) |
| Accsoon CineView Master 4K | The manual lists Apple Silicon MacBooks among wireless compatible devices, describes Accsoon SEE monitoring, and documents app-initiated RTMP/SRT pushing. Its receiver outputs HDMI/3G SDI. The transmitter's USB video output is described for iPhone/iPad. This does not establish generic RTSP, receiver UVC, or a redistributable Mac SDK. [Official manual, printed pp. 7-8 and 23-26](https://accsoon.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/CineView-Master-4K-User-Manual.pdf), [official specifications](https://accsoon.com/cineview-master-4k/) | Test the documented SEE monitoring route first, then whether app-initiated RTMP/SRT can reach a local Mac receiver with acceptable delay. Receiver HDMI/SDI capture is the fallback. Direct integration into our own Mac app still needs protocol/SDK evidence. |

For the two app-capable systems, success in a vendor app establishes a baseline, not access for a new app. The next useful proof is receiving live video and audio inside a small Mac prototype, either through a supported stream interface, a licensed SDK, or capture hardware. An app-mediated RTMP/SRT bridge is a candidate until local routing, destination support, and delay are measured.

Once a source works, use the same received feed for the main preview, a resizable always-on-top window, and optional sharing. The floating preview should have mute and a clear offline/reconnecting state. Avoid opening an extra transmitter connection for that window. This is a proposed design.

Record each tested profile's exact model/variant, firmware, Mac architecture, connection route, resolution/frame rate, audio, end-to-end delay, source-loss recovery, and interaction with existing receivers or vendor apps. Start one source at a time. A multi-camera promise needs a separate test because simultaneous transmitter Wi-Fi connections and receiver capture have different connection requirements.

Do not import the Mars 400S Pro's receiver-LAN recipe into the 300/300 Pro profiles, or the CineView HE/2 SDI's receiver-UVC capability into the Master 4K profile. The owned models have their own documented routes and unresolved integration questions.
